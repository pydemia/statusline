STATUSLINE

Settings

Configure a persistent browser status bar without changing page code.

Appearance

Use a comfortable browser-chrome height with clear interactive controls.

Clock

Set the date and time layout directly with a format pattern.

Tokens: YYYY, YY, MMMM, MMM, MM, M, DD, D, dddd, ddd, HH, H, hh, h, mm, m, ss, s, A, a. Put literal text in [brackets]. Use an IANA time zone such as Asia/Seoul. World clocks are managed by clicking the clock in Statusline.

Widgets

Drag ordinary widgets to change order. Shortcuts stay far left and status/actions stay aligned to the right.
